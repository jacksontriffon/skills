Line-by-line review of a draft.

## Rules

- [ ] **Present tense** — "She wins the deal"
- [ ] **Active voice** — "He closes the sale", not "The sale is closed by him"
- [ ] **Verbs carry the force** — "She sprints", not "She runs quickly"
- [ ] **Short sentences** — "The offer fails. We rebuild it. It works."
- [ ] **Few commas** — "He tries. He fails. He tries again."
- [ ] **Simple words** — "Use", not "Utilize". Third-grade reading level.
- [ ] **Say what is** — "Keep it short", not "Don't make it long"
- [ ] **One word per idea** — "Past history" → "History"
- [ ] **Cut what survives cutting** — "In order to win, you need to work hard" → "To win, work hard". Same meaning, so the cut stands.

## Report

Quote the line, show the fix. The fix is the finding — naming the flaw costs more words than repairing it.

The review ends when every sentence is accounted for: it passes all nine, or it appears in the report with its fix.

A cut holds only when the meaning remains the same.
