# Business Metrics

Every business is a money machine: ad spend in, profit out. Two tables and one ratio is the whole answer.

## Per 1,000 clicks

| Dial | Value |
|---|---|
| Ad spend | $600 |
| Cost per click | $0.60 |
| Click-to-paid | 1.76% |
| Price | $25/mo |
| Churn | 10%/mo |
| Running costs | 28.6% of revenue |
| Profit | $2,542 |

Ad spend = Clicks × Cost per click. Customers = Clicks × Click-to-paid. Tenure = 1 ÷ Churn. Profit = Customers × Price × Tenure × (1 − Running costs) − Ad spend.

Running costs are the costs that scale with revenue — payment fees, app-store cut, tax, hosting, delivery. Salaries, rent and tools are costs too; ask whether to include them, recommending against it so the machine stays a single multiplier.

Values are an example. Fill every row from what you are given, asking for all gaps in one question.
